AI Failure Museum

AI systems are becoming more commonly used in high-stakes contexts, but they can and do fail in ways that are harmful to people, systems, and the enviroment. The AI Failure Museum is a collection of case studies documenting real world examples of AI failures, with the goal of educating the public about the risks of depending on AI systems to make important decisions.
