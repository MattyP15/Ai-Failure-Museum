About the Museum

What is this?

The AI Failure Museum is a curated collection of real-world case studies where AI systems failed in ways that harmed people, communities, or the environment. Each exhibit documents what went wrong, why it happened, and what we can learn from it.

Why does it matter?

AI is increasingly used to make high-stakes decisions in healthcare, criminal justice, finance, and public safety. Understanding how these systems fail is essential for building better, fairer, and safer technology.

Who is this for?

Everyone. Whether you're a student, developer, policymaker, or just curious about AI, the museum is designed to make these failures accessible and educational. No technical background required.
