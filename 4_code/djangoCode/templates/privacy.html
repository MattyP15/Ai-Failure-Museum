Privacy Policy

What We Collect

When you create an account, we store your username, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password. We also store quiz responses, points, and badge progress linked to your account.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to power the AI Failure Museum experience: tracking your progress through exhibits, awarding points for quiz completions, and displaying badges you have earned. We do not sell, share, or distribute your information to any third parties.

Cookies and Sessions

We use session cookies to keep you logged in and to track unique exhibit views. These cookies are essential for the site to function and are not used for advertising or tracking across other websites.

Data Storage

All data is stored in our project database. As a university project, this site is intended for educational purposes and is not hosted on production-grade infrastructure.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of all your data at any time. This will permanently remove your account, quiz attempts, points, and badges.
