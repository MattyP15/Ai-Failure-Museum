Success Criteria 

//Evaluation questions I think we should consider 
* Comprehension - Do users understand the purpose of the website, and do they understand why ai systems failed in every artifact they read? 
* Reflection - Can users reflefct on what theyve learned (quizzes)
* Accessability  - Can users with disabilities use all core features
* Usability  - Can curators effectivly create and manage exhibits?
* Engagement - Do elements like points or badges increase engagement without trivialising the context?
