2. Flooding
Covers flood‑risk modelling, hydrological prediction, early‑warning systems, and mapping tools. Failures often involve outdated data, incorrect assumptions, or over‑confident visualisations.
3. Urban Planning
Includes land‑use classification, zoning support tools, transport modelling, and infrastructure risk assessments. Failures may arise from scale mismatches, missing socioeconomic context, or misinterpreted outputs.
4. Wildfire Risk & Management
AI used for fire‑risk prediction, smoke‑plume modelling, or satellite‑based detection. Failures can involve false positives/negatives, poor transfer to new regions, or miscommunication of uncertainty.
5. Agricultural & Land‑Use Monitoring
Systems for crop health detection, soil moisture estimation, yield prediction, or automated irrigation. Failures often relate to seasonal bias, sensor limitations, or models trained on non‑representative climates.
